# W202 legacy patch

Six selected exact factory combinations: C180 and C200, each five-speed manual / four-speed automatic before September 1995 and five-speed automatic August 1996–June 1997. Not all W202 motors. Standard sedan only; no Sport/Esprit lowered-body values. Standard-production dates, not pre-production, are used and narrowed to the stated configuration subperiod. 0–100 km/h values are directly published metric figures.

Two Mercedes-Benz Public Archive pages supply all figures; exact URLs and limitations are in evidence.json and per-row scopes. Optional-automatic mass remains null. The historic 80/1268/EEC 90/120/urban figures are not converted or averaged into combined consumption. No mirror's figures, photos or prose were copied.

Offline: `node analysis/codex/mercedes-legacy-72/build.mjs`, then `node analysis/codex/mercedes-legacy-72/validate.mjs`. Patch only: root appends trims by exact W202 identity; frozen Mercedes card and shared reports are untouched.
