# Izh research checkpoint, 2026-10-27

7 passenger/light-commercial families, 11 scoped body/drivetrain records. No technical engine versions have been asserted. Own Czech summaries distinguish Izh-produced 412 from the entire Moscow Moskvich model history, and Izh Kombi's liftback form from a conventional estate.

The current [factory model history](https://ladaizhevsk.ru/index.php?r=staticContent&uri=modelsHistory), its [archived company presentation](https://izh-auto.ru/downloadservlet?d=c&fileName=Presentation+IzhAvto+ENG.pdf), and the [factory Fabula archive](https://izh-auto.ru/Pressa/hist_21261.jsp) disagree on several initial/terminal production dates. The affected values remain null with specific review reasons: 412 end, 2125 start, 2715 van start/end, Fabula start/end. Contemporary [21 December 2005 reporting](https://motor.ru/news/izh-21-12-2005.htm) supports actual 27175 production, rather than an announcement alone.

408/434 were factory products under Moskvich identity; assembly of Lada and Kia models does not create additional Izh-badged lines. Experimental Izh13/14/19/2126T and poorly established Nika/Fabula AWD variants remain deferred. This initial pass is not an exhaustive conversion-body catalogue.

Photographs are partial Commons selections with inspected whole-car framing and exact API credits. Pickup photographs are attached to the pickup record rather than the superficially similar van. Unsearched or poorly categorised models remain null, without a claim that no suitable photograph exists.
