# Hudson source and scope notes — 27 October 2026

- [Hudson-Essex-Terraplane club handbook](https://www.yumpu.com/en/document/view/26298524/general-info-handbook-sect-1-hudson-essex-terraplane-club/15), the [historical account](https://www.americanautohistory.com/Marques/Marque760.htm), and the [Bonhams2015 catalogue](https://electronicsandbooks.com/edt/manual/Art/Auction/Bonhams/2015/20151210%20S-22705-0-1%20Collector%27s%20Motor%20Cars%2C%20Motorcycles%20and%20Automobilia%20%5B204%5D.pdf) establish early numbered production models. A1910 model year is distinguished from actual1909 production; Model33's October1910 introduction is not asserted as its exact production start.
- [US Federal Register, December1943](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1943-12-22/pdf/FR-1943-12-22.pdf) and [June1944](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1944-06-13/pdf/FR-1944-06-13.pdf) explicitly identify 112, CountryClub, DeLuxe and Custom model families and factory series. Their cited model years are **not** automatically calendar production boundaries. These families therefore have null production bounds until original factory records settle them. Traveller needs a fuller primary catalogue check and currently also has null bounds.
- SuperSix is split across its original,1933revival, prewar and postwar periods. The wartime gap is not filled with imaginary continuous passenger-car production.
- Actual Hudson-badged Rambler/Metropolitan are distinguished from the entire Nash/Rambler/Metropolitan histories. Essex and Terraplane are not wholesale copied into the Hudson brand. More precise local export badging and remaining historical annual-series identities still need work.
- Italia road coupes are distinguished from X-161 four-door development prototypes. Prototype photographs found in the same Commons category were rejected. Italia's exact production bounds remain unresolved against prototype/presentation/delivery years.
- Photo fallbacks are explicit: second-generation Hornet uses a whole-car rear three-quarter view; Jet uses a whole-car frontal view. Other selected images use front three-quarter perspectives. No image was downloaded as a product asset; temporary inspection caches are deleted.

This is a partial historical catalogue pass, not a claim that every annual factory code, coachbuilt body and light commercial derivative is complete.
