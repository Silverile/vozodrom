# Hongqi: source checks, 27 October 2026

This is a partial catalogue pass, not a claim that every special state vehicle or powertrain has been documented.

- [Manufacturer heritage](https://www.hongqi-auto.com/pages/history/history.html) confirms actual CA72 series production in 1958 and identifies CA771/CA773 as derivatives of CA770. [FAW chronology](https://www.faw.com/fawen/gyjt36/fzlc/index.html) distinguishes the extended limousine's 1965 introduction from CA770's 1966 official approval. No prototype date is silently treated as a production date.
- [Official regional manufacturer heritage](https://www.hongqi-ev.de/en/history.html) calls CA750 trial production. The [original academic historical study](https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/20798/1/Yihuai%20Wang%20PhD%20Thesis.pdf) says the Nissan-based CA750 and subsequent Ford/Dodge projects did not enter production. CA750 is therefore excluded from the final production cards.
- CA7560 and CA7560LH were a small development/display batch, not verified normal road production. The [contemporary retrospective](https://www.sohu.com/a/57192231_115822) describes about 20 examples and no formal series production. Excluded pending firmer road-production evidence; this does not mean no physical vehicles were built.
- The [museum investigation, part 3](https://carnewschina.com/2016/10/31/visit-to-the-hongqi-factory-museum-in-changchun-china-part-3-the-cars-that-i-didnt-see/) identifies an actual small series of CA7465 C8, 1995–1997, distinct from the later CA7460 programme and from a 2007 one-off retro CA7460 concept. That production family was included.
- The [CA1021 investigation](https://carnewschina.com/2012/02/18/history-the-amazing-hongqi-ca1021-audi-based-pickup-trucks-from-china/) confirms the pickup's 1993–1998 production and Audi-derived identity. It does not establish one engine or set of dimensions across the entire family.
- [Museum investigation, part 2](https://carnewschina.com/2016/10/17/visit-to-the-hongqi-factory-museum-in-changchun-china-part-2/) and the academic study distinguish individual Audi-derived factory codes from broader Century Star/Mingshi families. Their end dates differ; the affected line-level ends remain null. CA7460's Lincoln donor history is not imported as its Hongqi production history.
- The [global manufacturer's E-HS9 sheet](https://faw.web.cloud-top.com.cn/pages/cartype/cartype_E-hs9_new.html) explicitly lists Executive/Deluxe/Flagship/Long Range configurations. Motor power and torque are recorded as arithmetic sums of the two listed motors, without importing another market's motor ratings. WLTP ranges are manufacturer lower bounds (≥), explicitly stated in every version's scope. Height is contradictory even within the primary page: 1713 mm in the introduction versus 1731 mm in the table. All selected E-HS9 version heights therefore remain null. Another market's 400 kW rating is not substituted for this sheet's 160+245 kW.

Unfinished: detailed historical variants, facelift-specific dimensions, complete engine/gearbox tables, newer 2025/2026 products whose actual series start remains unverified, and additional whole-car Commons photographs. Current source years do not prove a current Czech-market offer.
