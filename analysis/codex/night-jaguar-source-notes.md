# Jaguar research checkpoint, 2026-10-27

31 road-production family cards, 61 scoped generation/body records. This is a broad initial historic pass, not an exhaustive engine, coachbuilder or trim catalogue. The 11 pre-existing Jaguar line slugs are preserved. SS1/SS2/SS90/SS100 are explicitly predecessor SS identities; XKSS continuation cars and race-only C-Type/D-Type are not silently added to ordinary road series.

The evidence JSON files preserve fetched individual model and generation pages. Dates describe their scoped production, not automatic confirmation that a car remains in production when `to` is null. Conflicting XJ Series II UK/South Africa end dates and unknown XEL/XFL dates remain null.

The two selected XE 163 PS diesel versions come from [Jaguar's 18 November 2015 EU technical release](https://media.jlr.com/archive-jaguar/news/2015/11/jaguar-xe-gains-all-wheel-drive-next-generation-infotainment-system-and-apple-watch). The manufacturer's automatic figures and bracketed manual figures are kept separate. Published kerb weights are minima (`from`), not guaranteed weights of every configuration. Width excludes mirrors. Combined EU consumption is retained without inventing a named test cycle. Drive remains null because the scoped technical table does not itself establish it. Other engine/gearbox combinations remain deferred.

Commons photos are selected by inspecting downloaded thumbnails and recording exact API author/licence metadata; rejected cropped shots are not used. The category search is partial; null photos are not a finding that no suitable free image exists.
