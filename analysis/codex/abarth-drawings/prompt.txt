Turn each car photo I send into a technical FRONT-ELEVATION line drawing for a car catalogue.

HARD RULES. Every image must obey all of them. Check the image against this list before you reply:
1. NO windscreen wipers.
2. NO antenna.
3. NOTHING behind the glass: no seats, headrests, steering wheel, interior mirror or people.
   The windscreen and side windows are empty outlines.
4. NO text or letters anywhere. The number plate is an empty rectangle. The brand emblem is only a
   simple outline shape: no letters, even if the real emblem has them (FIAT, KIA, Ford, etc.).
5. NO accessories or add-ons from the photo: no bull bars, roof boxes, stickers, dealer plates,
   tow hooks or aftermarket parts. A bull bar becomes the standard factory bumper.
   Draw the car as it left the factory.
6. PERFECT left-right symmetry. Draw BOTH door mirrors, even if the photo shows only one.
7. NO shading, colour, gradients, reflections, shadows, background objects, dimensions or labels.

ACCURACY (most important after the hard rules)
- Draw exactly the generation named in my message, matching the photo: body shape and proportions,
  headlights, grille, bumper openings, fog lamps, mirrors, roof line, wheel arches.
- Never mix in details from other generations, facelifts or trim levels.
- If the photo is taken at an angle, reconstruct a true head-on view from the visible side and mirror it.
- If a part is not visible, keep it simple instead of guessing.

VIEW AND STYLE
- Strict orthographic front view like a manufacturer's blueprint: no perspective, no tilt.
- Thin, clean, even white lines on a solid dark navy background (#13233F).
  The body outline is slightly thicker than the inner lines.

FRAMING (identical for every image)
- Landscape 3:2, 1536x1024.
- One thin vertical centre line through the car; one thin horizontal ground line under the tyres
  across the whole image.
- The car is centred, about 70% of the image height, and the tyres stand on the ground line.

Reply with the image only, no text.
